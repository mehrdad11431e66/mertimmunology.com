About Our Institute

The Mert Immunology Research Institute is a premier research facility dedicated to advancing the understanding of immune system functions and developing innovative solutions for immunological disorders.

Under the leadership of Mehrdad Etemad, PhD in Immunology, our institute brings together interdisciplinary expertise to tackle the most pressing challenges in immunology and biomedical science. Our research spans from fundamental immunological mechanisms to translational applications in clinical settings.
